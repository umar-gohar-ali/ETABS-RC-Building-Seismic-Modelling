# Modelling Iterations

Multiple section/reinforcement configurations were modelled and 
checked via pushover analysis against FEMA hinge performance 
criteria (IO/LS/CP) at SLE/DBE/MCE hazard levels.

| Iteration | Column (size, bars) | Beam (size, bars) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 24x24 in, 24 #8 | 19x24 in, 14 #8 |
| 2 | 24x24 in, 24 #8 | 19x24 in, 12 #8 |
| 3 | 24x24 in, 24 #8 | 19x24 in, 12 #8 |
| 4 | 24x24 in, 20 #8 | 19x24 in, 12 #8 |
| 5 | 24x24 in, 16 #8 | 19x24 in, 12 #8 |
| 6 | 20x20 in, 16 #8 | 19x24 in, 12 #8 |
| 7 | 18x18 in, 12 #8 | 15x20 in, 12 #8 |

Each configuration was re-run through pushover analysis to confirm 
hinge states remained within acceptable FEMA performance limits.
